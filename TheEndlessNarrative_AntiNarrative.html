Anti-Narrative

“Found in The Endless Narrative.”

  • Origin: It wasn't born or created; it is the physical manifestation of the author's block, discarded ideas, and the inevitable end of all stories. It is the void outside the margins.
  • Intelligence: Omniscient in terms of structure, but acts purely on the instinct to consume any narrative there is.

Abilities & Immunities

  • Absolute Narrative Consumption: It eats stories. It eats character development, backstories, and dialogue. When it attacks, entire paragraphs of the story disappear, leaving characters confused.
  • Chain-Layer Devouring: It can eat through infinite layers of narrative. If a story takes place inside a dream, inside a video game, inside a novel, it eats all of them simultaneously.
  • Authorship Immunity: No pen or paper can defeat it. If an author tries to write "And then the Anti-Narrative died," the Anti-Narrative simply eats the ink and words before they take effect. Immune to plot, fate, and narrative manipulation.
