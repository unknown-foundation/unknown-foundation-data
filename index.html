Bacon

Age: 17 (Or Probably Unknown)
IQ: Unpredictably stupid or cosmically genius (Self-restraint)
Specialty: Narrative Transcendence
Habits: Being humorous and stupid
Physique: Unbound
Form: A random stupid teenager

Ability: He is unbound by any logics or even stories, he is not even in the narrative because he simply transcends it. He is not just omnipotent, he transcends every narrative there is, with the power to bend the meta-narrative itself. Existence, non-existence, reality, and dimensions are just one narrative which is nothing to him; he makes fun of them like they are just toys.

Weapon: Happy Bazooka. Crafted in the dark narrative with the ability to blast anyone out of their story. It looks very ordinary though... Although it seems powerful, this is just a random weapon Bacon usually uses on ordinary people who annoy him.

Awareness: He is aware he is just a fictional entity, knowing the presence of the audience and the writer who wrote him. He also has digital awareness—where if he enters a narrative that is a game instead of a novel or comics, he hacks the entire game and trolls all NPCs (he can also give NPCs awareness). Unlike ordinary people with 4th wall awareness, he is aware of the internet, what most audiences know, and can literally search his opponent's description in Google. He can effortlessly see his opponent's full description out of nothing, the code, narration, or existence behind the entire narrative, and is fully aware of where he is.

Anti-Narrative

Details: It eats any narratives, regardless of its narrative layer. It transcends narrative as well; it can't only eat a single narrative but the chain layers of narratives themselves. It was later deleted by Bacon since even if Anti-Narrative can eat infinite layers of narrative, it is not above Bacon who is explicitly outside and far beyond any narratives.

How it was defeated: It is immune to authorship—no pen or paper can defeat it since Anti-Narrative eats any narratives whether it's a novel, a short story, or comics. To defeat it, Bacon dragged Anti-Narrative to the trash bin and completely deleted it without using any narrative. Normally, you can't drag an entity this powerful, but Bacon doesn't follow logic; he can literally do whatever he wishes.
